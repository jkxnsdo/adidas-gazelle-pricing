## Why the same Gazelle keeps showing up at different prices

Three separate markets sell the Gazelle at once, and they don't move together.

**Retail.** adidas sets the RRP. Gazelle Indoor colorways released in 2026 mostly landed at $130, the Lo Pro at $110 to $120, and the Wales Bonner takes at $200 to $220.

**Discounted retail.** Retailers run markdowns on slower colorways. Next's US site has listed Gazelle trainers at $105 against a "was" price of $163. Those aren't rare drops; they're the leftovers of last season's palette.

**Resale.** Editorialist's price history for one Gazelle Indoor listing tracked between $119 and $179 from January to June 2026, and a second Gazelle listing moved between $103 and $189, sitting at $115 in late May. On hyped pairs the spread gets extreme: Stadium Goods showed a $266 pre-release ask for the Bad Bunny Gazelle Indoor "Solar Gold" against its $140 retail price, pulled on 2 October 2026.

So if you're hunting a Gazelle, the first decision isn't colour. It's whether you're paying RRP for a pair that will restock, or waiting for a markdown on a colourway nobody else wants. Most people overpay by defaulting to RRP on a shoe that gets discounted within a quarter.

### The exclusions that quietly kill adidas cashback

These are the ones that cause the most rejected claims, taken from ShopBack's published adidas terms:

- **Purchases made through the adidas app** are excluded. The in-app checkout generally doesn't track. Use the website or a marketplace official store instead.
- **Orders paid with voucher codes** can void cashback entirely. Discounts and cashback don't always coexist on adidas.
- **Promo codes not featured on ShopBack** also sit outside the terms.
- **Fees, taxes, service charges and delivery** aren't included in the cashback calculation.
- **Returned, cancelled, refunded or exchanged items** earn nothing, and partial returns get adjusted proportionally.

That last cluster creates a real decision. If your market's adidas voucher is 20% off and cashback is 4%, take the voucher. If the voucher is 5% and cashback is upsized to 15%, skip the voucher. Compare the two numbers rather than stacking them blindly, because adidas often won't let you have both.
